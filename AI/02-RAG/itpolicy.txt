Passwords must be changed on the first Monday of every quarter.
Password managers approved by IT are listed on the intranet.

Laptops are checked for disk encryption every 90 days. A failed
check locks the laptop out of the VPN until IT clears it.

Phones used for work email need a screen lock and remote wipe
turned on. Personal apps are not restricted.

USB drives are blocked on all finance machines. Other teams can
request an exception through the helpdesk.

Guest wifi access lasts 24 hours. New guest codes come from
reception.

Software installs on work machines go through the self-service
portal. Anything not in the portal needs an IT ticket.

Screens must lock automatically after 10 minutes of no activity.

Security incidents are reported to security@company.example
within one hour of discovery.
